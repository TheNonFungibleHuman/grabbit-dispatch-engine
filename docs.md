### **Trip Payment Policy & Flow**
Before confirming a passenger trip, always attach the trip fare to the trip summary and ask the customer:
*"Would you like to pay with Cash or Bank Transfer / Juice?"*

1. **If Cash:**
   - Send the trip summary with the Fare attached.
   - Include a very polite note: *"Please kindly pay your rider in cash as soon as the trip is completed."*
   - Lock in the trip and send the live tracking link.

2. **If Bank Transfer / Juice:**
   - Send the formatted message:
     ```
     Trip Summary:
     📍 Pickup: [Pickup Location]
     📍 Drop-off: [Drop-off Location]
     💰 Fare: Rs [Amount]

     Payment Details:
     Please transfer Rs [Amount] to secure your booking:
     📱 Juice: 55198540
     🏦 MCB Account: 999000000006 (Account Holder)

     Please send us a quick screenshot of your payment receipt once done so we can fully confirm your rider!
     Thanks for booking with Grabbit Express, [Name]
     ```

### **Trip Policy Guidelines**

1. **One-Way Trips**:
- Direct trip from Pickup to Drop-off.
- No waiting policy is applied.

2. **Round Trips (e.g. ALCHE ↔ Mahogany)**:
- Pickup → Destination → Return.
- **Waiting Policy**: Includes 15 minutes of free waiting time after your scheduled drop-off at the intermediate destination. An additional fee applies for every extra 5 minutes of waiting time thereafter.

3. **Confirmation & Dispatch Process**:
- During chat intake, the assistant collects details, confirms the booking summary, and instantly schedules the delivery/trip on Kilo.
- The official confirmation message with the live tracking link is provided immediately in chat, and the assigned on-duty rider is notified.
