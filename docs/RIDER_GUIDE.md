# 🛵 Grabbit Fleet Rider AI Guide

Welcome to the Grabbit Fleet Rider System! The Grabbit AI acts as your 24/7 personal dispatch assistant on WhatsApp & Google Chat.

---

## ⚡ 1. How the AI Communicates with You

* **07:30 AM Morning Shift Briefing**:  
  Every morning at 07:30 AM, the AI sends you your exact on-duty shift hours for today and all advance pre-booked runs assigned to you.
* **Instant Booking Alerts**:  
  Whenever a customer books a trip or delivery during your shift, you receive an immediate alert with pickup location, dropoff destination, customer name, and scheduled time.
* **25-Minute Pre-Ride Reminders**:  
  25 minutes before every scheduled pickup, the AI sends you a reminder so you have ample time to navigate to the pickup point.

---

## 🚀 2. Fast 1-Word WhatsApp Commands (Instant, Zero Delay)

You can manage your status and active runs by texting simple 1-word commands on WhatsApp:

| Your Message | What the System Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Delivered`** or **`Done`** | Automatically marks your active run as completed, sends the customer a thank-you note, and frees you up for your next dispatch. |
| **`Break 15m`** or **`Break 30m`** | Logs a temporary break. The AI routes incoming orders to other on-duty riders during this time. |
| **`Back`** or **`Ready`** | Clears your break immediately and marks you active for new dispatches. |

---

## 💬 3. Live Customer & Dispatch Updates

You can text the AI naturally just like a colleague:

* **Customer Arrival Alerts**:  
  Text: *"I am at the ALCHE main gate"* or *"Here now"*  
  ↳ The AI immediately sends a WhatsApp alert to your customer (*"🛵 Update from your Rider (Thierry): I have arrived at ALCHE main gate"*).
* **Schedule Inquiries**:  
  Text: *"What is my schedule today?"* or *"Do I have any trips after 2 PM?"*  
  ↳ The AI replies with your exact upcoming runs and timings.
* **Customer Delays / Issues**:  
  Text: *"Customer is not answering their phone at Mahogany"*  
  ↳ The AI logs the note, alerts dispatch admins if needed, and advises on next steps.

---

## 🛡️ 4. What the AI Will NEVER Do
* ❌ The AI will **never** ask you to book a ride or treat you as a customer.
* ❌ The AI will **never** ask you for payment or pitch restaurant menus to you.
* ✅ You are recognized strictly as an **Authorized Fleet Rider on Duty**.
